Questions

Before we get started.

Why do you only accept two projects per month?

We intentionally limit ourselves to two projects per month so we can give every client the attention, care and expertise their business deserves. By keeping our client roster small, we can focus on the details, maintain the highest standards of quality and deliver the best possible results for the few companies we work with each month. We prioritize quality over quantity.

What do you need from us?

Your company details, service information, logo or brand assets, project photos and contact details. We handle the writing and design.

Will our website appear on Google?

We prepare the site for search discovery and submit the sitemap where access permits. Google controls indexing and rankings; neither immediate indexing nor a particular ranking is guaranteed.

Can you build a calculator or financing portal?

Yes, we can discuss advanced features separately. Custom calculators, package catalogs, portals and complex integrations are outside the standard five-page package.

What happens after seven days?

You review the site, request included revisions and approve it for launch. Launch timing depends on feedback and domain or hosting access.

Do we own the website?

The agreed website deliverables are handed over upon completion. Third-party software and services remain subject to their own terms.
